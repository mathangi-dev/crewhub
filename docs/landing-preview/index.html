How CrewHub works

Three steps, in order.

1. Organizers post an eventAdd venue, date, job categories, vacancies and the wage for each role.
2. Workers applyEligible applicants fill the vacancies in order. The rest join a waitlist.
3. Event day and paymentAttendance is tracked on site. The organizer pays each worker directly by UPI.

For workers

  • Find jobs near you by category, date and distance.
  • See your status: confirmed, waitlisted or offered.
  • Get wages from the organizer in full. CrewHub never deducts its fee from you.

For organizers

  • Set separate vacancies and wages for each role.
  • Replacements are offered to the waitlist automatically.
  • CrewHub charges a 10% platform fee on total wages, billed separately.

Frequently asked questions

How are workers selected?

Automatically, first come, first served among eligible applicants. Extra applicants join an ordered waitlist.

What happens if someone cancels?

The next waitlisted worker gets an offer with a deadline. If they decline or do not reply, it moves to the next person.

How do workers get paid?

The organizer pays each worker directly through UPI. CrewHub records the payment and its transaction reference.

Does CrewHub take a cut of wages?

No. CrewHub charges organizers a separate 10% platform fee. Worker wages are never reduced.
